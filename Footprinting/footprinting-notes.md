# Footprinting

## What is Footprinting?

Footprinting is the process of gathering information about a target before performing a security assessment or penetration test.

---

## Types of Footprinting

### Passive Footprinting

Passive footprinting collects information without directly interacting with the target.

Examples:
- Google Search
- WHOIS
- Social Media
- DNS Records

---

### Active Footprinting

Active footprinting collects information by directly interacting with the target.

Examples:
- Ping
- NSLookup
- Nmap
- Banner Grabbing

---

## Commands Practiced

### nslookup

Purpose:
Displays the IP address and DNS information of a domain.

---

### ping

Purpose:
Checks whether the target is reachable.
